アカウントとデータの削除 / Developer: ragdollp (SpringCat)

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Block Destroy and log in.
  2. Open ⚙️ Settings → 🗑️ アカウント削除 (Delete account).
  3. Confirm twice. Your account and the server data below are deleted immediately.

アプリでログインし、⚙️設定 →「🗑️ アカウント削除」を押して2回確認すると、すぐに削除されます。

Request deletion on the web

If you can no longer use the app, email nsa310754@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject “Block Destroy account deletion”, and include your username if you have one. We will delete your account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

アプリを使えない場合は、アカウントのメールアドレスから上のアドレスへ件名「Block Destroy account deletion」で連絡してください(ユーザー名があれば記入)。30日以内に削除して返信します。

What is deleted

  • Login account (email address)
  • Cloud save, username, world-ranking entries
  • VS and event records, device registrations linked to the account
  • Comments you posted (on request by email; they can also be reported/removed individually)

What may be kept

  • Purchase records required by law and by Google Play (we never hold card details)
