Privacy Policy — Block Destroy

Last updated: 2026-11-02 / 日本語の要約は各項目の下にあります

1. Overview

Block Destroy can be played offline without an account. Some optional features (login, cloud save, world rankings, comments, online VS, purchases) and the services that keep the app running (ads, analytics, notifications, security) send data to Google services. This page explains what is collected, why, and how to delete it.

ログインなしでも遊べます。ログイン・クラウドセーブ・世界ランキング・コメント・オンライン対戦・購入などの機能と、広告・分析・通知・不正防止のために、Google のサービスへデータが送られます。

2. Data stored on your device

Scores, achievements, settings, unlocked modes and in-progress games are stored locally on your device. They can be deleted at any time with “Reset All Data” in the game or by clearing the app’s data.

記録・実績・設定などは端末内に保存され、ゲーム内の「全データリセット」で消せます。

3. Data we collect and why

  • Account (optional): email address and login ID (Firebase Authentication), used to sign in and protect your account.
  • Game data you choose to send (optional): username, submitted scores, cloud save, VS / event records (Firebase Realtime Database).
  • Comments (optional): the text and username you post are public to other players. You can report and block other players.
  • Device identifier: the Android ID and an in-app device ID, used only to limit accounts per device and prevent abuse. They are not used for advertising.
  • Purchases: purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive the product ID and purchase status to deliver items. We never receive your payment card details.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob may use the advertising ID and device information to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings. Monthly Premium removes ads.
  • Analytics: Google Firebase Analytics collects app usage events and device information to improve the game.
  • Notifications: a Firebase Cloud Messaging token, used to send daily-bonus and event notifications you allow.
  • Security: Firebase App Check (reCAPTCHA Enterprise) checks that requests come from the real app.
  • AI mode (optional): if you enter your own Gemini API key, the board state is sent from your device directly to the Google Gemini API. The key is stored only on your device.

メールアドレス(ログイン時)・ユーザー名とスコア・クラウドセーブ・コメント(公開)・端末ID(不正防止のみ。広告には使いません)・購入の記録(カード情報は受け取りません)・広告ID(AdMob)・利用状況(Firebase Analytics)・通知用トークン・不正防止の確認情報を扱います。

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is processed by Google (Firebase, AdMob, Google Play, Gemini API) as our service providers. Comments and world-ranking names/scores are visible to other players. Data is encrypted in transit.

データを販売しません。Google のサービスで処理されます。コメントと世界ランキングは他のプレイヤーに公開されます。通信は暗号化されています。

5. Retention and deletion

Account data is kept until you delete your account. To delete your account and the server data linked to it, open ⚙️ Settings → 🗑️ アカウント削除 (Delete account) in the app, or request deletion on the web: https://springcat.ragdollp.site/delete-account/. Requests are handled within 30 days. Purchase records may be kept as required by law and by Google Play.

アカウントは、アプリの ⚙️設定 →「🗑️ アカウント削除」、または上のページから削除を依頼できます(30日以内に対応)。購入の記録は法律と Google Play の決まりにより保管する場合があります。

6. Children

This game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. In-app purchases require confirming you are 18 or older.

13歳未満を対象としていません。アプリ内購入は「18歳以上」の確認が必要です。
