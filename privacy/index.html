Privacy Policy — Block Destroy

Last updated: 2026

1. Information We Do Not Collect

This game (Block Destroy) does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. Core game data (scores, achievements, settings) is stored locally on your device using localStorage and never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the optional cloud-save or world-ranking features by logging in.

2. Local Storage

This game uses local storage on your device to save:

  • High score rankings
  • Unlocked achievements
  • Game statistics (total blocks destroyed, items used, etc.)
  • Unlocked game modes and settings

This data is stored on your device and can be deleted at any time using the “Reset All Data” button in the game.

3. Optional Online Features (Login)

If you choose to log in with an email address, the game uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) to provide optional features such as world rankings, comments, and cloud save. In that case, a username, scores you submit, and a device identifier used for anti-abuse device limits may be stored on Firebase. You can play the entire game offline without logging in.

4. Advertising

This game may display advertisements served by third-party advertising networks (e.g. Google AdSense). These networks may use cookies or similar technologies. Please refer to the respective ad network’s privacy policy for details.

5. Analytics

The app may use Google Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate, anonymous usage. No personally identifying gameplay data is sold or shared with third parties.

6. Notifications

With your permission, the app can show daily-bonus reminders and event notifications (via Firebase Cloud Messaging). You can disable notifications at any time in the app settings or your device settings.

7. Children’s Privacy

This game does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
